SIMPLE THREADS — MESHY AI PROMPTS FOR THE CLOTHING RAIL
=======================================================

HOW TO MAKE ONE MODEL (do ONE test first: Plain Hoodie - Ash Grey)
1. In Meshy, choose "Image to 3D".
2. Turn on multi-image / multi-view if offered, and upload the FRONT and BACK
   pictures of one item (caps: FRONT and SIDE). Only one item per model.
3. Settings:
   - Style / mode: Realistic
   - Texture: ON (PBR if offered)
   - Symmetry: Auto
   - Polycount / target: about 20,000–30,000 (keeps the website fast)
   - Topology: Triangle is fine (quad not needed)
4. Paste the matching prompt below into the prompt / texture box.
5. When it's done, turn the model around and check the back and the sides.
   If it looks good, download it as GLB and send it to me.
6. Only then do the other pieces (credits cost money).

WHAT TO CHECK BEFORE DOWNLOADING
- Shape: shoulders, sleeves and hem look like real fabric, not a flat board.
- Details match the photo: collar, cuffs, pockets, drawstrings, zips, snaps,
  stripes. No extra pockets, zips or logos added.
- Colour matches the photo.
- No mannequin, no person, no stand inside the garment.
If something is wrong, try "Regenerate" once, or tell me what looks off.
